Haoran Zhu

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at New York University, working on world models with Prof. Yann LeCun.

My ultimate vision is to build intelligent machines for the physical world. To enable that long term vision, I'm interested in world models, self-supervised learning, and end-to-end learning.

Previously, I earned my Ph.D. from New York University as well, where I was advised by Prof. Anna Choromanska and worked on deep learning for autonomous driving.
